Add spatial effects to your audio with the reverb processor.

## Reverb Presets

Choose from built-in reverb presets that simulate different acoustic environments:

- Room (small, medium, large)
- Hall
- Cathedral
- Telephone effect
- Custom impulse responses

## Controls

- **Dry/Wet Mix**: Balance between the original signal and the reverb effect
- **Preset Selection**: Quick-switch between reverb types
- **On/Off Toggle**: Enable or disable reverb processing

## Custom Presets

Create and save your own reverb presets with custom parameters. Save them for instant recall.

## Per-Track Settings

Like all Chorus audio effects, reverb settings can be saved per track for automatic recall.

## Combined with Other Effects

Reverb works alongside the [Equalizer](/features/equalizer/) and [MS Processor](/features/ms-processor/) in the audio effects chain. Save combined settings as audio presets.
