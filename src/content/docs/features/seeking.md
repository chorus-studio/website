Customize your fast-forward and rewind behavior with configurable seek durations.

## How It Works

Chorus adds seek controls to the Spotify player that let you jump forward or backward by a configurable number of seconds.

## Content-Specific Defaults

Seek durations can be configured differently based on content type:

| Content Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **General (G)** | Music tracks — typically shorter seek intervals |
| **Podcasts (P)** | Podcast episodes — usually 15-30 second intervals |
| **Audiobooks (A)** | Audiobook chapters — customizable to your preference |

## Custom Durations

Set your own seek duration for each content type. For example:

- 5 seconds for music (skip an intro)
- 30 seconds for podcasts (skip ads)
- 15 seconds for audiobooks (rewind to catch something)

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Both seek forward and seek backward are available as keyboard shortcuts. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](/features/keyboard-shortcuts/) for details.
