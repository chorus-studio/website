The MS (Mid-Side) audio processor provides advanced stereo imaging and spatial effects.

## What is Mid-Side Processing?

Mid-Side processing separates audio into:

- **Mid**: The center/mono content (vocals, bass, lead instruments)
- **Side**: The stereo content (reverb tails, panned instruments, spatial effects)

By adjusting the balance between mid and side, you can dramatically change the spatial characteristics of any track.

## Effects

The MS processor supports multiple effects that can be applied independently to the mid and side channels:

- Stereo width control
- Channel balance
- Multi-effect processing chains

## Use Cases

- **Widen the stereo image**: Boost the side channel for a more immersive sound
- **Focus on vocals**: Boost the mid channel to bring vocals forward
- **Create ambient mixes**: Reduce mid and boost side for a spacious, atmospheric feel
- **Karaoke-like effect**: Reduce the mid channel to attenuate centered vocals

## Presets

Save your favorite MS processing configurations as presets for quick access.

## Part of the Audio Chain

The MS processor is part of the full audio effects chain alongside the [Equalizer](/features/equalizer/) and [Reverb](/features/reverb/). All three can be saved together as an audio preset.
