We're excited to launch the official Chorus website at [chorusstudio.org](https://chorusstudio.org)!

## What's Here

- **Documentation**: Comprehensive guides for every Chorus feature, from snips to audio effects
- **Downloads**: Get Chorus for Chrome, Firefox, or download directly from GitHub releases
- **Changelog**: A full history of every release
- **Blog**: Announcements, feature spotlights, and tips (you're reading it now!)

## Why a Website?

As Chorus has grown from a simple snip-saving tool to a full audio enhancement suite with EQ, reverb, mid-side processing, and 20+ keyboard shortcuts, we needed a proper home for documentation and community updates.

The Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons listings are great for discovery, but they're limited in what we can communicate. This website gives us room to provide detailed guides, announce new features, and share tips for getting the most out of Chorus.

## What's Next

We'll be publishing feature deep-dives, tips & tricks, and release announcements here. If you have suggestions for content you'd like to see, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/chorus-studio/chorus/issues).

Thanks for using Chorus!
